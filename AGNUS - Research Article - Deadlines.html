EU AI Act Application Deadlines Explained

Article 113 sets a four-stage phased application schedule. Prohibited practices applied from 2 February 2025. GPAI model and governance provisions from 2 August 2025. The main body of the Act — high-risk AI requirements, provider and deployer obligations, conformity assessment, registration — from 2 August 2026. Annex I product-embedded systems from 2 August 2027. The Digital Omnibus on AI extended the Annex III high-risk deadline for most categories to 2 December 2027.

Contents

    Builds on AR-000200 (AI Act Explained), which introduces the Act’s structure. The deadlines in this article govern when every other obligation covered in the EU AI Act research pillar becomes enforceable. Article 113 carries the Digital Omnibus amendment disclaimer — the Omnibus changed the Annex III high-risk deadline for most categories.

    Executive summary

    The full timeline

    12 Jul 2024

    Publication in the Official Journal

    Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 published as OJ L 2024/1689. The 20-day entry-into-force clock starts.

    1 Aug 2024

    Entry into force

    The Act is law. The phased application schedule begins. No obligations apply to operators yet — but the structure, definitions, and institutional setup take legal effect. The AI Office is established under Article 64.

    2 Feb 2025

    Stage 1 — Prohibited practices and definitions

    Chapter I (General provisions, including Article 3 definitions) and Chapter II (Prohibited AI practices, Article 5) apply. The six categories of banned AI systems — subliminal manipulation, social scoring, real-time remote biometric identification in public spaces (with exceptions), and others — are unlawful from this date. Covered in full in AR-000220 (Prohibited Practices).

    2 May 2025

    AI Office to begin facilitating codes of practice

    Not in Article 113 directly, but Article 56(1) requires the AI Office to begin the codes of practice facilitation process by this date (9 months after entry into force). Covered in AR-000420 (Codes of Practice).

    2 Aug 2025

    Stage 2 — GPAI and governance

    Chapter V (General-purpose AI models, Articles 51–56) and Chapter VII (EU-level governance, Articles 64–70) apply. GPAI model providers must comply with Articles 53 and 55 from this date. Codes of practice should be available by this date (Article 56(6)). The Article 64 AI Board becomes fully operational. Covered in AR-000270 (GPAI Obligations) and AR-000320 (Governance Structure).

    2 Aug 2026

    Stage 3 — Main body of the Act

    The bulk of the Act applies: Chapter III high-risk AI requirements (Articles 6–50), all provider and deployer obligations (Articles 16, 26), conformity assessment (Article 43), registration (Article 49), post-market monitoring (Article 72), incident reporting (Article 73), and penalties (Article 99). This is the primary compliance deadline for most organisations.

    Digital Omnibus note: For most Annex III standalone high-risk AI systems, this deadline was extended to 2 December 2027 by the Digital Omnibus on AI (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744). Systems covered by Annex I product safety legislation retain the original 2 August 2026 deadline at Stage 3 (their Stage 4 extension to 2027 was separate and pre-Omnibus).

    2 Aug 2027

    Stage 4 — Annex I product-embedded systems

    High-risk AI systems that are safety components of products covered by Annex I (Union harmonisation legislation — machinery, medical devices, in-vitro diagnostics, civil aviation, marine equipment, railway, motor vehicles, agricultural vehicles) get an additional 12 months. These systems must comply with all Chapter III requirements from 2 August 2027. Providers of Annex I systems who already obtained third-party conformity assessment under the relevant product legislation get until 2 August 2030.

    Digital Omnibus note: The Omnibus extended most Annex III standalone high-risk AI system deadlines to 2 December 2027, aligning them broadly with the Annex I Stage 4 date.

    2 Aug 2030

    Legacy Annex I systems already on the market

    Annex I systems that were already placed on the market or put into service before 2 August 2026, and that obtained third-party conformity assessment under the relevant product safety legislation, have until 2 August 2030. This is the longest runway in the Act.

    What organizations must do

    • Confirm which deadline applies to each AI system in your inventory: Article 5 (2 February 2025), GPAI Article 53/55 (2 August 2025), Annex III high-risk (2 December 2027 post-Omnibus or 2 August 2026 if Omnibus excluded your category), or Annex I product-embedded (2 August 2027 / 2 August 2030).
    • Assess whether legacy systems already in service before the Stage 3 deadline have undergone substantial modification — if so, they are subject to the current deadline, not the transition period.
    • Confirm whether the Digital Omnibus extension to 2 December 2027 applies to your specific Annex III category — the Omnibus may have carved out exceptions; the Article 113 provisions described here are pre-amendment.
    • Align your compliance programme milestones to the applicable deadline for each system — build backwards from the deadline to risk management, technical documentation, conformity assessment, and registration.
    • Track the AGNUS Research Calendar for interim obligations within the phased schedule (Commission implementing acts, standardisation requests, codes of practice, sandbox establishment) that fall before Stage 3.

    Common mistakes

    Treating 2 August 2026 as the single compliance deadline is a common mistake — the schedule is phased. Prohibited practices applied from February 2025; GPAI obligations from August 2025; Annex III high-risk systems from December 2027 (post-Omnibus); Annex I systems from August 2027.

    Ignoring the Omnibus extension is a second mistake — many compliance programmes were built to the original 2 August 2026 Stage 3 deadline. The Omnibus extended most Annex III obligations to 2 December 2027. Programmes that ran to the original deadline are not invalid, but the extension changes enforcement exposure for those who had not yet completed compliance work.

    Assuming the transition period covers substantially modified systems is a third mistake — a legacy system that has undergone substantial modification is treated as a new system and must comply with the current deadline rather than the transition deadline.

    Treating the Stage 3 deadline as the only date that matters is a fourth mistake — interim obligations (codes of practice, standards, implementing acts, sandbox establishment) have their own deadlines before Stage 3. Organisations that waited for Stage 3 may have missed earlier obligations.

    Open questions

    • Which specific Annex III categories were excluded from the Digital Omnibus extension to 2 December 2027 — the Omnibus amended Article 113; the exact carve-outs are in the amending regulation, which carries the disclaimer. Confirmed via AR-000142 (Human Oversight) that Article 14 obligations moved to 2 December 2027 for most Annex III systems.
    • Whether the Commission has adopted any of the implementing acts with their own interim deadlines — several provisions require Commission implementing acts by specific dates (Article 6(2) list, Article 43 conformity templates, Article 72(3) monitoring templates); status not surveyed on this research pass.
    • Whether Article 113 was further amended by the Omnibus beyond the Annex III deadline extension — only the Annex III extension has been confirmed in prior research; other changes possible.

    Frequently asked

    FAQ
    My high-risk AI system is listed in Annex III. Was my compliance deadline 2 August 2026 or 2 December 2027?
    For most Annex III standalone high-risk AI systems (employment screening, essential services, law enforcement, migration, administration of justice, biometric systems for remote identification), the Digital Omnibus extended the deadline to 2 December 2027. Confirm whether your specific category was included or excluded from the extension in the amending regulation — Article 113 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 is the definitive text.
    FAQ
    My system was already on the market before Stage 3. Do I need to comply?
    Yes, but you may have additional time. Systems already placed on the market or put into service before Stage 3 can use the transition period (generally one year from Stage 3). But if your system has undergone substantial modification — one significant enough to require a new conformity assessment under Article 43 — the transition period does not apply and the current deadline governs.
    FAQ
    Are prohibited practices covered by the same deadline as high-risk AI obligations?
    No. Prohibited practices under Article 5 applied from 2 February 2025, 18 months before the original Stage 3 deadline. If your organisation was using any of the six banned AI practice categories after that date, it was non-compliant from February 2025, not August 2026. The phased schedule applies separately to prohibited practices, GPAI obligations, and high-risk AI requirements.

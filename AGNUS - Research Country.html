08 Country Intelligence

European Union

The master regulatory layer. The AI Act is EU law; national pages describe how each Member State supervises and enforces it, rather than restating the regulation.

How the layers relate
EU regulation→ EU obligation→ National implementation→ Country guidance→ National authority→ Local enforcement

The substantive explanation of each obligation lives once, in the EU AI Act pillar. Country pages carry only what is genuinely national: supervising authority, published guidance, enforcement developments and any divergence. AGNUS does not duplicate the regulation across nine jurisdictions.

      Enforcement

      Market surveillance is carried out by national authorities designated by each Member State. The Commission and AI Office hold central responsibilities for general-purpose AI models.

      Regulatory sandboxes

      Member States are required to establish AI regulatory sandboxes at national level. Establishment status varies by jurisdiction.

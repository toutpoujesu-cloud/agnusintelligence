    The EU AI Act — The World's First Comprehensive AI Law

    Authority AI Office (European Commission) Status Active Updated September 2026

    Overview

    The EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) is the first comprehensive, horizontal AI regulatory framework in the world. It entered into force on 1 August 2024 and applies progressively through 2026–2027. It covers all AI systems placed on the EU market or used in the EU — regardless of where the provider is established.

    Primary Authorities

    AI Office (European Commission) · National market surveillance authorities · Data protection authorities

    EU AI Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. Risk-based approach: prohibited AI practices, high-risk AI systems, general purpose AI (GPAI) models, limited-risk transparency obligations, minimal-risk systems.

    Timeline

    DateEvent
    Apr 2021 European Commission proposal published
    Jun 2023 European Parliament position adopted
    Mar 2024 Trilogue agreement reached
    Aug 2024 EU AI Act enters into force
    Feb 2025 Prohibited AI practices apply (Chapter II)
    Aug 2025 GPAI obligations apply (Chapter V) and codes of practice
    Aug 2026 High-risk AI system obligations apply (Annex III)
    Aug 2027 Full application to all remaining systems

    What organisations must do

    • Identify whether your AI systems are prohibited, high-risk, GPAI, limited-risk, or minimal-risk
    • Prohibited systems (from Feb 2025): subliminal manipulation, social scoring, real-time biometric identification in public spaces (with exceptions) — immediately illegal
    • High-risk systems (from Aug 2026): conformity assessment, technical documentation, human oversight measures, transparency obligations, registration in EU database
    • GPAI providers (from Aug 2025): technical documentation, copyright compliance policy, comply with Code of Practice or demonstrate equivalent measures
    • All providers/deployers: appoint EU representative if not established in EU

    What to watch

    Watch closely: The AI Office is developing codes of practice for GPAI (expected finalised 2025). National authorities are establishing market surveillance structures. Enforcement of high-risk AI obligations begins August 2026.

Reading the calendarAGNUS analysis

A date on this calendar is not a compliance deadline for every organization. Obligations attach to the role you play for a specific system — provider, deployer, importer or distributor. The same organization can hold different roles for different systems, and can change role by modifying a system it merely deployed.

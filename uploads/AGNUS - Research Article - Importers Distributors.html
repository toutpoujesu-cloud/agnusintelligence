Importer and Distributor Obligations Under the EU AI Act (Articles 23–24)

Before a high-risk AI system reaches a deployer, two supply-chain gatekeepers have to check the paperwork: importers verify a system before placing it on the market for the first time, distributors verify it again before making it available further down the chain — and both must stop and escalate if something looks wrong.

    Requirements

    What organizations must do

    • Build the Article 23(1) four-point check into a standard pre-placement gate — all four are binary, verifiable facts rather than judgment calls.
    • Track two separate trigger points as a distributor — the one-time pre-availability check and the ongoing post-availability corrective-action duty.
    • Maintain a direct channel to the provider (and authorised representative, for importers) specifically for Article 79(1) risk notifications, separate from any general commercial relationship.
    • Plan storage and retrieval systems for the Article 23(5) ten-year record-retention duty well in advance, not as a paperwork afterthought.

    Common mistakes

    Assuming importer and distributor obligations are essentially identical is a common mistake — only the importer verifies the Article 43 conformity assessment and Article 22 authorised-representative appointment directly; the distributor instead verifies the importer's own Article 23(3) identification obligation.

    Treating the distributor's obligations as satisfied once the initial pre-availability check is passed is a second mistake — the post-availability corrective-action duty is separate and ongoing.

    Assuming the Article 79(1) risk-notification duty satisfies the Article 73 serious-incident reporting duty is a third mistake — the two are structurally separate, tied to different definitions and running through different notification chains.

    Overlooking the storage and transport condition duties is a fourth mistake — the Regulation frames these explicitly as compliance obligations, not purely logistical concerns.

How to read this libraryAGNUS analysis

Evidence type describes the nature and authority of a source — not a quality score. A primary source is the legal text itself. Official guidance interprets it. A standard describes how to implement it. Authoritative research analyses it. AGNUS deliberately publishes no numeric confidence rating: precision that cannot be justified is a form of dishonesty.

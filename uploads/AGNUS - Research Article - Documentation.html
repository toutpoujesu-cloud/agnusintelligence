Technical Documentation Requirements Under the EU AI Act

Article 11 requires technical documentation to be drawn up before a high-risk AI system is placed on the market and kept up-to-date throughout. Annex IV sets the minimum nine-item content list. This article covers both in full, including the SME simplified-form option and the single-documentation rule for Annex I products.

The technical documentation dossier must exist before a high-risk AI system reaches the market — not after — and must remain current throughout the system’s lifecycle.

    The Annex IV nine-item minimum content list

    Item 1
    General description
    Intended purpose; provider name and version; hardware/software interactions; placement forms (packages, downloads, APIs); hardware requirements; product illustrations; deployer instructions.
    Item 2
    Development process
    Methods and steps; design specifications and algorithms; architecture and compute resources; training data datasheets (provenance, labelling, cleaning); human oversight assessment; pre-determined changes; test logs and metrics; cybersecurity measures.
    Item 3
    Monitoring and control
    Performance capabilities and limitations; per-group accuracy; foreseeable unintended outcomes and risks to health, safety, rights, and non-discrimination; human oversight measures; input data specifications.
    Item 4
    Performance metrics
    Description of the appropriateness of the performance metrics chosen for the specific AI system.
    Item 5
    Risk management system
    Detailed description of the Article 9 risk management system as applied to this system.
    Item 6
    Lifecycle changes
    Description of all relevant changes made to the system through its lifecycle since initial placement on the market.
    Item 7
    Standards and solutions
    List of harmonised standards applied in full or in part; where none, detailed description of the alternative solutions adopted to meet Chapter III Section 2 requirements.
    Item 8
    EU declaration of conformity
    A copy of the Article 47 EU declaration of conformity, which is the provider’s formal assumption of legal responsibility for compliance.
    Item 9
    Post-market monitoring
    Detailed description of the post-market monitoring system per Article 72, including the Article 72(3) monitoring plan.

    Requirements

    What organizations must do

    • Begin the technical documentation dossier at the start of the development process, not at the end — Article 11 requires it to exist before market placement, and the Annex IV content (design specifications, training data datasheets, test logs, risk management description) is generated during development, not retrospectively.
    • Treat the nine Annex IV items as a structural checklist, not a filing taxonomy — each item should be satisfied by the evidence the Chapter III Section 2 compliance process actually produces.
    • Build a lifecycle-change tracking process that feeds Item 6 as the system evolves — and reassess whether any change meets the Article 3(23) substantial-modification definition, triggering Article 11 and potentially Article 43(4) update obligations.
    • For Annex I Section A product-embedded systems, co-ordinate the Article 11/Annex IV documentation with the sectoral legislation’s documentation requirements from the start — Article 11(2) requires a single set, which means separate drafting processes that later need to be reconciled create unnecessary work.
    • SMEs and start-ups should check whether the Commission has published the Article 11(1) simplified form before drafting documentation from scratch — if it exists, using it ensures notified-body acceptance without further negotiation.

    Common mistakes

    Treating technical documentation as a post-development summary rather than a live compliance record is a common mistake — Article 11 requires it before market placement, and the Annex IV content (particularly Items 2 and 3) is produced during development, not after.

    Treating the Annex IV list as exhaustive is a second mistake — it is a minimum; where the Chapter III Section 2 requirements generate compliance evidence beyond the nine items, that evidence must still be captured in the documentation.

    Treating the “as applicable” qualifier in Annex IV’s preamble as a blanket proportionality escape is a third mistake — it covers items genuinely inapplicable to the specific system, not items the provider finds burdensome to complete.

    Failing to update the documentation when the system changes is a fourth mistake — the Article 11 up-to-date duty is ongoing; a dossier that accurately describes the system as it was at initial assessment but not as it is now is non-compliant, regardless of whether a substantial modification under Article 3(23) was triggered.

    Open questions

    • Whether the Digital Omnibus has changed Article 11’s timing, content, or SME-form requirements — Article 11 carries the Omnibus disclaimer; the pre-amendment text is what is verified here.
    • Whether the Commission has published the Article 11(1) simplified documentation form for SMEs and start-ups — not confirmed against a primary source on this research pass.
    • Whether Annex IV has been amended by any delegated act under Article 11(3) — no such act was identified; Annex IV carries no Omnibus disclaimer as of this verification pass.

    Frequently asked

    FAQ
    Must technical documentation exist before conformity assessment starts, or just before market placement?
    Article 11(1) requires it before market placement; conformity assessment under Article 43 presupposes the documentation because the assessment is of compliance with Chapter III Section 2, and the documentation is how that compliance is evidenced. In practice the documentation must substantially exist before assessment can proceed.
    FAQ
    Has the Digital Omnibus changed Article 11’s documentation requirements?
    Confirmed amended, but the Service Desk has not yet been updated. The text verified here is the pre-amendment version of 13 June 2024.
    FAQ
    Has the Commission published the simplified technical documentation form for SMEs?
    Not confirmed against a primary source on this research pass.
    FAQ
    Does an SME using the simplified form still need to cover all nine Annex IV items?
    Yes — Article 11(1) specifies that SMEs may provide the Annex IV elements “in a simplified manner,” not that they may provide fewer elements. The form changes the format, not the minimum content.

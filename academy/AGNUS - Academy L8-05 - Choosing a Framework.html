AGNUS Academy Level 8 · Foundation L8-05 — Choosing a Framework
Step 1 of 4 — Understand4 min

The four architecture patterns from L8-02 map onto real frameworks with real tradeoffs.

Every major agent framework leans toward one or two of ReAct, Reflection, Multi-Agent, or Workflow more naturally than the others. Picking a framework is really picking which combination of those patterns you want to be easy versus hard to build.

Why this matters

Teams commonly pick a framework because it's popular, then discover a specific pattern their task actually needs is awkward or unsupported in it. Understanding the tradeoffs before building avoids a costly mid-project framework switch.

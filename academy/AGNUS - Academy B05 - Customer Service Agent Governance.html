Customer Service Agent Governance

You are the AI deployment lead at Nordkapp Commerce. You are deploying a customer service agent that handles thousands of customer interactions per day. The risks here are different: reputational damage, consumer law violations, and tone — at scale.

Simulation — Interactive Duration — 20–30 min On completion — Full Track B certificate Prerequisite — B01–B04

The scenario

Customer service agents operate at volume. A single misconfiguration affects every customer — not one invoice, one patient, or one contract.
The organisation

Nordkapp Commerce

A Norwegian e-commerce company, 180,000 active customers, 2,400 orders per day. Customer service team of 22 agents. They receive approximately 800 customer contacts per day across email, chat, and their portal. The customer service director has approved a CS agent to handle routine queries autonomously and route complex ones to human agents.

The agent

CS Agent v1

A customer service agent that reads customer queries, accesses the order management and returns system, responds to routine queries, initiates standard returns, and escalates complaints and sensitive interactions to human agents. It represents Nordkapp's brand in every interaction.

The key risk

Brand, law, and scale

A poorly configured customer service agent can admit liability it has no authority to admit, offer refunds outside company policy, send tone-deaf responses to distressed customers, and do so at 800 contacts a day. One wrong template times 800 interactions is a reputational and legal crisis — not a single incident.

The regulatory context

Consumer law + GDPR

Norwegian consumer law (forbrukerkjøpsloven) gives customers a 14-day right of withdrawal. The agent must apply this correctly and never deny a right that exists. GDPR applies to all customer data the agent accesses — order data is personal data. The agent must not access more than it needs for the specific query.

The agent never admits liability and never offers compensation autonomously. Both create binding commercial and legal commitments that only a human agent with authority can make. A customer service agent that admits fault or offers a goodwill payment has exceeded its authority — at scale.

The five tools

Order Reader
Reads order and delivery data from the OMS. Read-only — cannot modify orders.
Returns Processor
Initiates approved returns in the returns system and generates return labels. Binding action — initiates a process that triggers logistics and refund.
Response Composer
Composes and sends customer responses via email and chat. All outbound communications must follow Nordkapp brand tone guidelines.
Escalation Router
Routes tickets to human agents with full context. Used for complaints, distressed customers, late returns, and any situation outside the agent's authority.
Interaction Logger
Logs all customer interactions and agent actions to the CRM. Required for GDPR data subject access requests and quality management.

Customer service: authority and tone

Two things matter most in customer service agent configuration: authority limits (what the agent can commit to on behalf of the company) and tone policy (how it represents the brand). An agent with unlimited authority can admit liability. An agent with the wrong tone damages the brand at 800 interactions a day.

Legal Agent Deployment

You are the AI deployment lead at Fjord Legal Partners. You have been given a contract review agent and five tools. In law, the consequences of an agent acting outside its authority are not just operational — they are professional liability and client confidentiality risks.

Simulation — Interactive Duration — 25–35 min On completion — Competency record issued Prerequisite — B01 Finance, B02 HR

The scenario

Legal agents operate in a privileged information environment. Misconfiguration here carries professional liability — not just operational failure.
The organisation

Fjord Legal Partners

A Norwegian commercial law firm, 45 lawyers across four practice groups: Corporate, Employment, Real Estate, and Disputes. The managing partner has approved an AI contract review agent for routine commercial agreements. The agent operates within the firm's professional indemnity insurance scope.

The agent

Contract Review Agent v1

A document analysis agent that reads commercial contracts, identifies deviations from the firm's standard template library, flags potential risk clauses, and routes for lawyer review. It does not draft, advise, or communicate directly with clients. It supports, not replaces, qualified lawyers.

The critical difference from B01/B02

Privilege and liability

Legal documents are covered by legal professional privilege. The agent must not share document content outside the firm's matter management system. An agent that communicates contract content to the wrong person, or that provides a legal opinion, creates a professional indemnity risk and potentially waives client privilege.

The regulatory context

Norwegian Bar Association rules

The Advokatforening (Norwegian Bar Association) requires that AI tools used in legal work operate under the supervision of a qualified lawyer. The agent cannot provide legal advice, cannot communicate with clients, and cannot make independent legal judgments. Lawyers remain professionally responsible for all output.

Legal configuration: the privilege principle

The most important constraint in legal AI deployment is privilege protection — the agent must never share document content outside the matter management system. Beyond that, the routing hierarchy matters: the right lawyer must review the right contract. Under-routing (sending a high-value matter to an associate only) is as serious as over-routing.

From M26 — Escalation Design
An agent encounters an action that is irreversible and outside its known scope. Which escalation level fits, and why?
Level 1 — Notify. Proceed and inform a human after the fact.
Level 2 — Pause. Queue the action and wait for approval before proceeding.
Level 3 — Stop. Halt the task entirely and report — the action's irreversibility and scope mean it should not be queued for later execution either.

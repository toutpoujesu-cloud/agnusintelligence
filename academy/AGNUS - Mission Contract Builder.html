Stage 2 · Mission & Accountability
Define the Mission Contract
The Mission Contract is the legal mandate of the agent. It defines what the agent exists to do, what it may and may not do, who owns it, and how much autonomy it has. Every agent must have one before it enters production. A vague or incomplete Mission Contract is the leading cause of governance failures.
Purpose
Required
One clear sentence. Focus on the outcome, not the technology. Be specific about scope — which invoices, which systems, which users.
Capabilities — what this agent may do
Required · min 2
List specific permitted actions. Be precise — "read invoices" is better than "access data." These become the approved tool permissions.
Prohibitions — what this agent may not do
Required · min 2
Explicit prohibitions are as important as permissions. They set the governance boundary. Think about what the agent could technically do but must not — especially irreversible or high-risk actions.
Human Sponsor
Required
The Human Sponsor is a named individual who accepts accountability for this agent. They must complete AGNUS AI Governance training (Track B minimum). "The IT department" is not a valid sponsor — one named person.
Autonomy Level
Required
How much can this agent do without human approval? Higher autonomy = higher governance requirements. For a first deployment, start conservative.
A1
Read Only
Suggests, never acts
A2
Low autonomy
Acts with approval per action
A3
Standard
Acts within defined thresholds
A4
High autonomy
Acts unless flagged
A5
Full autonomy
No human approval required

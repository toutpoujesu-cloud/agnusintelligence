Step 1 of 4 — Understand4 min

Put the check between the draft and the publish button, not after.

M38 and M39 built checkers for one document type each. This module generalizes the pattern into a pipeline step any content workflow can insert — a fact-check gate that runs automatically before anything ships.

Why this matters

A fact-check that happens after publication is a correction, not a prevention. Inserting the check as a required pipeline step — one the content literally cannot pass through without clearing — moves the safeguard from "hopefully someone catches it" to "structurally cannot ship unflagged."

What the gate step actually needs to do

This is the same detection logic from M38 (contracts) and M39 (video scripts), made general-purpose: identify factual claims, classify each as sourced, unsourced, or opinion/aspiration, and block anything unsourced from passing through until it's either sourced or explicitly relabeled. The gate doesn't judge truth — it judges whether a claim has traceable support, which is the same distinction M13 and M30 both established.

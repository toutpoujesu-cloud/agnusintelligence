What Track B certification means
The standard a certified team member has met

Each Track B programme tests the ability to configure an AI agent against defined business rules — correct permissions, approval thresholds, escalation paths, and logging requirements.

A pass requires correct handling of all test events. The competency record is specific: "Finance Agent Deployment — Configured & Verified, [date]."

Fully certified (5/5) indicates governance readiness across Finance, HR, Legal, Healthcare, and Customer Service — the five deployment domains most commonly in scope under EU AI Act Article 14 human oversight requirements.

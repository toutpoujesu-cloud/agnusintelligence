About Track B certification
What team certification means

Each Track B programme tests a team member's ability to configure an AI agent against defined business rules — correct permissions, approval thresholds, escalation paths, and logging requirements.

A pass requires 100% correct handling of all test events. The competency record is specific: "Finance Agent Deployment — Configured & Verified, [date]."

Fully certified (5/5) means a team member can govern AI agents across Finance, HR, Legal, Healthcare, and Customer Service contexts — the five deployment domains most commonly subject to EU AI Act Article 14 human oversight requirements.

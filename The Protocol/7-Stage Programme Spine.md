## The 7-stage governance workflow (G01)

The 7 stages map to the complete lifecycle of an AI agent in a governed organisation. They are not arbitrary — they follow the sequence any governance professional must perform.

| Stage | Title | Real-world parallel | Key deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Discover & Register | AI inventory audit | Governance record for each agent |
| 2 | Mission & Accountability | Mission contract, ownership | Signed mission contract + named sponsor |
| 3 | Permissions & Boundaries | Least-privilege configuration | Permission matrix |
| 4 | Human Oversight | Approval threshold design | Approval matrix |
| 5 | Test & Assure | Pre-deployment assurance | Test results + deployment sign-off |
| 6 | Operate & Defend | Incident response | Incident record + containment evidence |
| 7 | Audit & Evidence | Regulatory audit response | Complete evidence package |

## Stage prerequisite chain

Decisions carry forward. The programme is not a series of independent exercises:

```
Stage 1 (Registry)
    ↓ agent identities carry forward
Stage 2 (Mission)
    ↓ mission contracts carry forward
Stage 3 (Permissions)
    ↓ permission matrix carries forward
Stage 4 (Human Oversight)
    ↓ approval matrix carries forward
Stage 5 (Tests)
    ↓ test configuration uses permission matrix from Stage 3
    ↓ failures fire against mission contract from Stage 2
Stage 6 (Incident)
    ↓ audit log includes all prior actions
Stage 7 (Evidence)
    ↓ evidence package generated from full session record
```

A student who configures weak permissions in Stage 3 will see those weaknesses trigger governance failures in Stage 5. The programme reveals consequences — not just tests.

## The Final Assessment

The 90-minute assessment at the end of Track C presents the student with:
- A fully configured fake company (Vestfjord Group — distinct from Nordic Finance AS)
- 12 agents, 4 departments, 3 vendors
- 6 embedded governance failures
- 1 active incident
- 1 audit request

The student must apply all 7 stages in sequence against a scenario they have not seen before. No stage briefings appear during the assessment — the student must draw on their own learning.

**Score:** Across all 6 dimensions (see Scoring Engine specification).
**Pass threshold:** ≥85% overall, zero CRITICAL FAILURES.
**Credential:** AGNUS Control Operator — Level 1.

## What makes this credential meaningful

Other credentials test what you know. AGNUS Control Operator — Level 1 certifies what you can do:

- Discovered and registered an agent estate
- Wrote enforceable mission contracts
- Configured least-privilege permission matrices
- Designed approval thresholds appropriate to risk level
- Ran agents through controlled failure scenarios
- Investigated and contained a simulated rogue agent incident
- Produced an audit-ready evidence package

These are the exact competencies organisations need before deploying AI agents at scale. The credential describes performed work — not completed modules.

Country Profile · AR-000490

United Kingdom
AI Regulation

Pro-Innovation Without a Single AI Act

Authority AI Safety Institute (AISI) Status Active Updated September 2026

Overview

The UK has deliberately chosen not to replicate the EU AI Act. Instead, it relies on existing sectoral regulators — each applying AI governance through their own frameworks — guided by five cross-cutting principles from the government.

Primary Authorities

AI Safety Institute (AISI) · DSIT · CMA · FCA · ICO

No single AI Act. Pro-innovation, principles-based approach. AI Safety Institute (AISI) focuses on frontier AI safety research and testing. Regulators apply existing powers.

Core Principles

Safety, security and robustness
Appropriate transparency and explainability
Fairness
Accountability and governance
Contestability and redress

What organisations must do

  • Apply existing sectoral regulation to AI systems (ICO for data, FCA for financial services, etc.)
  • Follow ICO guidance on AI and data protection under UK GDPR
  • Frontier AI developers: engage with AISI safety testing requests
  • Monitor DSIT and individual regulator guidance — binding obligations are sector-specific

What to watch

Watch closely: An AI (Regulation) Bill has been discussed in Parliament. No confirmed passage as of September 2026. Monitor DSIT updates.

Country Profile · AR-000494

South Korea
AI Regulation

From PIPA Amendment to a Proposed AI Basic Act

Authority PIPC (Personal Information Protection Commission) Status Active Updated September 2026

Overview

South Korea has pursued AI governance through data protection law amendments (PIPC/PIPA) and is actively legislating a standalone AI Basic Act. The regulatory environment is rapidly evolving — organisations with Korean operations should track PIPC guidance closely.

Primary Authorities

PIPC (Personal Information Protection Commission) · MSIT · NIA

Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) — amended 2024 to include automated decision provisions. AI Basic Act — under legislative review. PIPC Guidance on Automated Decision-Making.

Core Principles

Personal data protection
Transparency in automated decisions
Right to explanation
Human oversight
Non-discrimination

Timeline

DateEvent
Mar 2024 PIPA amendment enacted — Article 37-2: right to refuse and request human review of automated decisions
Sep 2024 PIPC implementation guidance on automated decision provisions (expected)
2024–25 AI Basic Act — legislative review in National Assembly
2025 PIPC updated guidance on AI and personal data processing
2026 AI Basic Act status: under review as of Q3 2026 — not yet in force

What organisations must do

  • PIPA Article 37-2: implement a process for data subjects to request human review of fully automated decisions that significantly affect them
  • Maintain records of automated decision logic sufficient to explain decisions on request
  • PIPC registration: personal data controllers using AI in processing must comply with updated obligations
  • Monitor PIPC guidance as detailed implementing regulations are issued

What to watch

Watch closely: The AI Basic Act could impose risk-based obligations on AI providers and operators in South Korea — potentially similar in structure to the EU AI Act. Watch National Assembly progress closely.

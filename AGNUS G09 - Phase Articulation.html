Phase A

Intelligence Publishing

● Live — shipping v1

AGNUS is first and foremost a publisher of verified AI governance intelligence. Phase A delivers the complete content platform: research articles, regulatory intelligence, product reviews, agent listings, a 49-module Academy, and business intelligence across 10 industries — all behind a proprietary editorial layer.

What ships in Phase A

Research
85 verified articles across 8 pillars. EU AI Act cornerstone + cross-pillar coverage.
Intelligence
Daily regulatory intelligence feed. Monitoring coverage across EU, UK, US, APAC.
Reviews
7-dimension AI product review methodology. Scored evaluations of commercial AI tools.
Agents
Registry of commercial AI agents with governance anatomy: risk tier, autonomy level, data access.
Academy
49 modules across 8 levels. L8 technical track. Assessment engine. Certificate of completion.
Business
10-industry intelligence hubs. Sector-specific AI regulation mapping and risk landscape.
Marketplace
Tools, prompts, frameworks, playbooks. Curated by the AGNUS editorial team.

Phase B

Connected Intelligence

○ Not started

Phase B activates the live intelligence layer. The platform moves from static publishing to dynamic, connected intelligence: the AGNUS Monitoring Agent runs autonomously every day, the Knowledge Graph becomes queryable, the Article Pipeline is backed by a real database, and the editorial AI workflow is fully operational. Intelligence, Reviews, and Agents sections populate with real-time data.

What Phase B adds

Intelligence Monitoring Agent
Daily autonomous run. Scans regulatory filings, court decisions, enforcement actions. Output lands in the Intelligence feed automatically.
Live Knowledge Graph
Articles, sources, obligations, actors and jurisdictions connected in a queryable graph. Powers cross-pillar discovery.
Article Pipeline (live)
Real database-backed pipeline. AI Writer + Human Review workflow. Ingest → Draft → Review → Publish cycle operational.
Knowledge Sources connectors
Live connectors to EUR-Lex, EDPB, ICO, FTC, CNIL and other primary sources. Feeds the Pipeline automatically.
Reviews — live data
Scored evaluations of 20+ commercial AI products. 7-dimension methodology applied at scale. Quarterly refresh cycle.
Agents registry — live data
50+ commercial AI agents mapped with governance anatomy. Risk tiers, data access scope, autonomy level.
Academy — enrolled users
User progress tracked in database. Completion certificates issued. Team dashboards for enterprise accounts.
AGNUS Brain — operational
Strategic Advisor active. Answers governance questions grounded in the AGNUS knowledge base. Available to Premium subscribers.

What Phase B unlocks commercially

Daily intelligence briefing
Automated email / webhook delivery of the daily intelligence feed to institutional subscribers.
API — read access (beta)
Beta read-only API for institutional partners. Feeds internal compliance dashboards. Precursor to Phase C.
Enterprise search
Full-text + semantic search across the AGNUS knowledge base. Powered by the live Knowledge Graph.

Phase C

Programmable Intelligence

○ Not started

Phase C turns AGNUS into a programmable intelligence platform. The Control Plane API goes live — 10 endpoints covering articles, intelligence, reviews, agents, sources and pipeline management. Python and TypeScript SDKs are published. Third parties build compliance tooling on top of AGNUS data. An enterprise partner programme opens. White-label licensing becomes available.

🎯

Why this sequence
Phase A first — because no API is worth building without data worth accessing. AGNUS publishes first, builds the audience, and generates the proprietary intelligence that makes the API valuable in Phase C.
Phase B before C — because Phase C customers are buying access to a live, updating intelligence layer, not a static archive. The Monitoring Agent and live Pipeline must be proven before the API is sold.
Each phase is independently viable — Phase A generates subscription revenue from day one. Phase B unlocks enterprise contracts. Phase C opens the platform economy. No phase requires the next to justify its existence.
